At Luxe Queue SaaS, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the data processed through our live queue and counter management system.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate the queue, including customer names provided during token generation, selected service catalogs, customer feedback/reviews, and salon operational metrics (revenue and served counts).

2. How We Use Your Data

Collected data is used strictly for real-time queue synchronization, estimated wait-time calculations, browser notification alerts when your turn arrives, and salon performance analytics visible to authorized salon owners.

3. Data Security & Storage

All authentication PINs, live queue states, and customer check-ins are securely handled. We implement robust multi-tenant isolation so that data remains strictly partitioned between different registered salons.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local browser storage and session cookies to maintain theme preferences, active token passes, and owner login states for seamless user experience.
