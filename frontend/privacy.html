Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 24, 2026

Welcome to Luxe Queue SaaS. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any minimal information processed through our digital queue management system.

1. Information We Collect

To provide queue tracking and salon management services, we process temporary details such as customer names, selected service preferences, feedback reviews, and basic salon operational data. We do not collect or store sensitive financial records, credit card numbers, or government identification documents.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is strictly utilized to generate active digital queue passes, estimate waiting times, display customer reviews on local dashboards, and enable salon owners to manage daily operational flow.

3. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard security measures (such as PIN authentication and token-based validation) to safeguard your workflow. However, no digital transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data to outside entities. All data remains strictly confined to the respective salon's operational scope.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the system administrator.
