At Luxe Queue SaaS, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the data processed through our live queue and counter management system[cite: 10].

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate the queue, including customer names provided during token generation, selected service catalogs, customer feedback/reviews, and salon operational metrics (revenue and served counts)[cite: 10].

2. How We Use Your Data

Collected data is used strictly for real-time queue synchronization, estimated wait-time calculations, browser notification alerts when your turn arrives, and salon performance analytics visible to authorized salon owners[cite: 10].

3. Data Security & Storage

All authentication PINs, live queue states, and customer check-ins are securely handled. We implement robust multi-tenant isolation so that data remains strictly partitioned between different registered salons[cite: 10].

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local browser storage and session cookies to maintain theme preferences, active token passes, and owner login states for seamless user experience[cite: 10].
